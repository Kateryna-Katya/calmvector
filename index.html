Calm vector: direction field

Concept

You’ve gotta think ahead about where you’re sending stuff.
Calm Vector: Direction Field is a grid-based strategy puzzle built around motion control.
The board is filled with directional arrows that define how particles travel.
You’re kinda working behind the scenes, but honestly, every single move you make has consequences.
You rotate or place vectors to guide particles safely to their targets.
The system follows logic. Your job is to read it.

Core Mechanics

Adjust the field.

  • Rotate directional arrows on a grid

  • Place new vectors in limited spaces

  • When you get it right, there's this moment where everything just works. Honestly, it's pretty damn satisfying when you nail it.

  • Complete levels within a fixed number of moves

  • Guide moving particles to specific targets

  • Particles obey the arrows strictly. If the field is poorly aligned, they collide, loop endlessly, or lose energy.
